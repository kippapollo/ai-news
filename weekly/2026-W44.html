The Financial Times reported Saturday morning that OpenAI pared its latest primary round from up to $30 billion to about $20 billion at a $1.25 trillion post-money valuation after setting a Friday deadline for investor commits, down from the $1.4 trillion number reporters had been running since September. CNBC's Ashley Capoot and Lora Kolodny confirmed in the afternoon; TechCrunch's Anthony Ha confirmed from the Wall Street Journal. The WSJ says OpenAI has secured roughly $11 billion so far and that the initial close will land below the revised $20 billion target, with a planned retail tranche aimed at wealthy individuals filling the remainder. Investors from the United Arab Emirates lead alongside Coatue, a composition CNBC first reported October 22.

The round sits inside a week where the AI capex trade got repriced across the whole equity complex. Alphabet reported Q3 after Wednesday's close with revenue of $127.6 billion, Google Cloud at $30.71 billion up 103 percent year over year, operating income of $40.58 billion up 30 percent, and a $561 billion cloud backlog, before shedding more than $280 billion of market cap on Thursday. GAAP EPS at $2.12 missed the roughly $3.02 adjusted consensus thanks largely to an $8.2 billion unrealized mark on SpaceX; free cash flow was negative $9.1 billion on $51.4 billion of Q3 capex against $55.09 billion expected. CFO Anat Ashkenazi told the call 2027 capex would "increase significantly" without giving a dollar number; Fortune framed the Street's working number at roughly $300 billion.

"Google's AI Mode and AI Overviews does not drive traffic for anybody."— Steve Huffman, Reddit CEO, Q3 2026 earnings call

The ancillaries got hit hardest

Reddit cratered 29 percent after Steve Huffman told the Q3 call Reddit had been losing more than 1 million weekly Google referrals on average by October. ServiceNow fell 27 percent on Thursday, its worst day since 2012, on $1.00 adjusted EPS versus $1.02 consensus and 21 percent subscription growth to $3.988 billion against a soft Q4 guide. Microsoft fiscal Q1 2027 printed $92.3 billion in revenue up 18.8 percent with Azure up 48 percent constant currency to $33.16 billion, but capex at $39.37 billion came in $6 to $11 billion below Street models and 2026 capex was trimmed to $170 billion from $175 billion. Amazon posted Q3 EPS of $1.89 against $1.97 consensus even as AWS grew 39 percent year-over-year to about $46 billion against a $578 billion backlog. Intel's guidance miss on Thursday after close, with Q4 revenue of $15 to $16 billion versus $17.1 billion expected and Q4 adjusted EPS of 25 cents against 43 cents, drove its worst single-day drop since the 1970s at minus 29 percent. Nvidia closed minus 4 percent, Micron minus 5.5 percent, the Philadelphia Semiconductor Index minus 5.7 percent. The Nasdaq posted its worst month since March 2025.

"The funding round is still in progress and has not closed, so the figures are subject to change."— Person familiar with the OpenAI round, cited by WSJ

Oracle will deploy Google TPU 8t and TPU 8i on OCI, the first time Google's TPUs run outside Google

The Decoder · 2026-10-28 · Source ↗

Each eighth-generation chip delivers up to 12.6 PFLOPS of native FP4, over 100x TPU v1 at 92 TFLOPS, with 2x previous-gen performance per watt and up to 80 percent better perf-per-dollar for low-latency serving. Oracle Acceleron carries SmartNIC tenant isolation, High Performance Fabric and Zero Trust Packet Routing. The same keynote added Cerebras wafer-scale accelerators, OpenAI's Jalapeño inference chip, and SambaNova SN50 into OCI alongside Nvidia Vera Rubin.

Google and Oracle used Tuesday's Oracle AI World keynote in Las Vegas to break a decade of single-cloud TPU distribution: Oracle Cloud Infrastructure will begin deploying Google's eighth-generation TPU 8t for training and TPU 8i for inference in calendar 2027 under a multi-year, multi-gigawatt partnership. The chips had never before run on a hyperscale cloud outside Google. Each eighth-gen chip delivers up to 12.6 PFLOPS of native FP4, more than 100x TPU v1 at 92 TFLOPS, with 2x previous-gen performance per watt, up to 80 percent better performance per dollar for low-latency serving, and sustained goodput above 97 percent across thousands of chips thanks to Optical Circuit Switching and on-die Inter-Chip Interconnect that reroutes around faults without job restarts.

TPU racks plug into Oracle Acceleron with SmartNIC tenant isolation, High Performance Fabric and Zero Trust Packet Routing. The initial deployment footprint was not disclosed. The same Tuesday keynote extended Oracle's existing Nvidia work toward multi-gigawatt Vera Rubin, added Cerebras wafer-scale accelerators, said OCI will host OpenAI's internal inference chip Jalapeño, and announced SambaNova SN50 deployment inside Oracle data centers. The combination makes Oracle the first hyperscaler running at least five accelerator families in production.

"This is a multi-year, multi-gigawatt partnership with Google. The eighth-generation TPU on Oracle is a first for the industry, and it sits alongside the Vera Rubin, Cerebras, OpenAI Jalapeño and SambaNova deployments we've announced this week."— Karan Batta, Oracle SVP, Oracle AI World keynote

Google Cloud CEO Thomas Kurian framed the economics

Google Cloud CEO Thomas Kurian said during the following day's Alphabet Q3 call that nearly 100 customers use TPUs today and that TPU 8t and 8i deliver up to 2.7x better training performance and 80 percent better inference price performance against the previous generation. He named the Oracle partnership and the Blackstone-backed Crux AI joint venture as the first two customer-site deployments. The TPU 8t/8i stack sits inside Google Cloud's $30.71 billion Q3 revenue (up 103 percent year-over-year) and the $561 billion cloud backlog.

"Nearly a hundred Google Cloud customers use TPUs today, and the eighth generation delivers up to 2.7x better training performance and 80 percent better inference price performance. The Oracle deployment is one of two customer-site builds we announced this week."— Thomas Kurian, Google Cloud CEO, Q3 2026 call

The structural fact is that Google's TPU program is no longer a vertical pillar reserved for first-party Google workloads. Opening the chip to a competing hyperscaler flattens the hyperscaler-silicon stack: Microsoft is in the market for Vera Rubin, OCI is now in the market for Vera Rubin plus TPUs plus three other accelerator families, AWS is pushing Trainium 3, and Meta brought Nvidia, AMD and Broadcom in as equity participants rather than exclusively as suppliers in its $95 billion raise the same week. The era in which a model company picked a cloud for the silicon it had, rather than the silicon it chose, is ending.
