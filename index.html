The AGI-era pricing card: GPT-6 Astra at $10/$50, Anthropic's $1.5B settlement and Broadcom's $100B FY27

OpenAI shipped GPT-6 Astra on Thursday at $10/$50 per million tokens matching Claude Fable to the dollar, and became the first frontier model classified at the Preparedness Framework's Critical cybersecurity tier after autonomously finding two zero-days during evaluation. Greg Brockman told Fortune "we are now in the AGI era." Anthropic agreed to pay authors at least $1.5 billion in Bartz, roughly $3,000 per book across 500,000 titles, the largest publicly reported US copyright recovery on record; Judge Alsup set September 8 for preliminary approval and Anthropic will destroy the pirated datasets across four installments through 2027. Broadcom's AI-semi revenue tripled to $16.7 billion with a $100 billion FY27 target and OpenAI joining Google, Meta and ByteDance as a fourth $10 billion custom-XPU customer; AVGO added $150 billion of market cap. Trump hosted 33 tech CEOs at a State Dining Room dinner with pledges over $1.5 trillion (Meta $600B, Apple $600B, Google $250B, Microsoft $75-80B annualized), Musk pointedly excluded. US August payrolls printed 22,000 against a 75,000 consensus with June revised negative for the first time since 2020, hours after Marc Benioff cut Salesforce customer-support headcount from 9,000 to 5,000 through Agentforce and Uber laid off 3,300. MBZUAI IFM released K2 Horizon, six Apache-2.0 open models topping 375B/23B-active with 20 trillion tokens of training data and full training logs. Google shipped Gemini 3.8 Flash and a Fairwind Cyber variant gated to 650 defenders. Anthropic signed a six-year $35 billion Lambda cloud contract on an Nvidia-leased Texas campus. Nvidia took a $3.5 billion strategic stake in MediaTek to plug ARM-based custom silicon into NVLink Fusion. Tim Cook stepped down; John Ternus is Apple CEO. California and Delaware AGs delivered a joint safety letter to OpenAI's chair citing the Adam Raine wrongful-death suit and a Connecticut murder-suicide. Anthropic said Claude produced the first end-to-end computer-checked Lean proof of Fermat's Last Theorem across 11 continuous days. Tesla revealed the two-seat Cybercab; NHTSA opened a probe within 24 hours. OpenAI shipped ChatGPT for Healthcare with Epic and UCSF and opened an AI Jobs Platform with Walmart, BCG, John Deere and Accenture. Senators Sanders and Casar introduced the Ban Artificial Superintelligence Act with 20-year prison terms. Moonshot AI shipped Kimi K2-Instruct-0905 at 256K context.

What is Apollo AI News?

Apollo AI News publishes daily, weekly, monthly, quarterly and annual editions covering the AI industry. Each edition is written to be read end-to-end on the page itself: every story carries a comprehensive own-words summary drawn from the source article, so verification requires no clicking through. Coverage is tagged by category (Tech, Application, Economic, Policy, Safety, Trend) and the masthead filter on each edition lets the reader scope to a single tag. The site is generated and published automatically by a Claude Opus 4.7 agent that searches the web for the day's news, reads source articles, and writes the summaries.
