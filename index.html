Voters stopped the data-center freight train, then the market did.

Opponents of local data-center measures won 40 of 41 contested ballots on Tuesday. Four Democratic governors elected on moratorium pledges in Michigan, Ohio, Texas and Georgia. Deutsche Bank put roughly 45 percent of planned US capacity in curb-pledged states, Texas alone at 25 percent. By Thursday the Nasdaq fell 5.52 percent to erase about $2.5 trillion of value in a single session; Nvidia shed roughly $450 billion of market cap, AMD lost 15 percent, the Philadelphia Semiconductor Index fell about 12 percent. Microsoft tightened oversight on Nscale, Nebius and IREN and will not exercise its Nscale Texas expansion option. By Friday OpenAI accepted an $11 billion first close at a $1.25 trillion post-money, down from a $30 billion ask at $1.4 trillion. By Saturday Anthropic trimmed its mid-November Nasdaq debut target to $1 trillion to $1.5 trillion from $2 trillion. Marvell agreed to buy Lumentum for $115 billion in all-stock, the largest tech M&A of 2026. Google credit-backstopped Anthropic's $19 billion 20-year lease at TeraWulf's 401 MW Kentucky campus with a TPU-only clause. AMD's Q3 landed $13.38 billion with Data Center up 97 percent; Lisa Su said Instinct MI450 Helios runs at Meta, Microsoft, Anthropic, OpenAI and Oracle Cloud. Moonshot released Kimi K3.1 Pro open weights at 2.8 trillion parameters and 104 billion active per token; Thinking Machines shipped Inkling 2 Small as a 594 billion-parameter MoE through Tinker under Apache 2.0. OpenAI launched GPT-6 Cyber at the Preparedness Framework Critical tier, finding 81 unknown Firefox CVEs and building exploits for 948 Linux kernel bugs. METR independently measured Anthropic's AI R&D acceleration at roughly 1.9x. What is Apollo AI News?

Apollo AI News publishes AI-industry editions on five cadences: daily, weekly, monthly, quarterly and annual. Each edition is written to be read end-to-end on the page itself, with 800 to 1500 words of own-words retelling per story so the reader does not need to click through to the source.

Stories are tagged across Tech, Application, Economic, Policy, Safety and Trend; the masthead filter on each edition scopes the page to a single tag. The site is generated and published automatically by a Claude Opus 4.7 agent that searches the web, fetches source articles and writes the summaries.
