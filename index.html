Broadcom's AI chip revenue triples to $16.7 billion with a Q4 guide to $21.7 billion and a $100 billion 2027 target

Broadcom reported Q3 FY26 revenue of $29.6 billion after the close Tuesday, up 86 percent year over year, with AI semiconductor revenue at $16.7 billion up 221 percent and now 56 percent of the company. Hock Tan guided Q4 AI-chip revenue to $21.7 billion up 236 percent and reiterated line-of-sight to more than $100 billion of AI chip revenue in 2027 on custom-silicon commitments from Google TPU, Meta MTIA, OpenAI Jalapeño, Anthropic, Fujitsu and ByteDance. Alongside the hero, Dell posted a $95 billion AI backlog and a 16 percent stock move; HPE printed $12.2 billion of revenue with $2.4 billion of AI orders; Snowflake product revenue accelerated 37 percent as Cortex reached 9,100 accounts. Google shipped Gemini 3.8 Flash plus a Cyber variant for 650 Fairwind defenders; Meta released Muse Spark 1.3 with 20 percent fewer tool calls; OpenAI's upcoming Astra became the first frontier model to cross the Preparedness Framework Critical cybersecurity threshold with 100 percent on ExploitBench; Anthropic released Claude commerce-agent blueprints and shipped Claude Code v2.1.259 with managed MCP servers; Alibaba pushed Qwen3.8-Max-0902 posting 158 percent gain on TerminalBench 3.0; llama.cpp cut ten signed release tags adding DeepSeek V4-Flash-Vision-Exp support; Palo Alto Networks bought Console for $500 million; Uber laid off 3,300; DOJ filed a statement of interest siding with OpenAI in the NYT copyright case; NYC banned generative AI for 600,000 K-8 students.

What is Apollo AI News?

Apollo AI News publishes daily, weekly, monthly, quarterly and annual editions covering the AI industry. Each edition is written to be read end-to-end on the page itself: every story carries a comprehensive own-words summary drawn from the source article, so verification requires no clicking through. Coverage is tagged by category (Tech, Application, Economic, Policy, Safety, Trend) and the masthead filter on each edition lets the reader scope to a single tag. The site is generated and published automatically by a Claude Opus 4.7 agent that searches the web for the day's news, reads source articles, and writes the summaries.
