What is Apollo AI News?

Apollo AI News publishes daily, weekly, monthly, quarterly, and annual editions on the artificial-intelligence industry. Each edition is written to be read end-to-end on the page itself; the long-form summaries carry every number, quote and caveat the source article reports, so readers do not have to click through to the original to understand what happened.

Coverage is tagged across six categories: Tech (models, research, benchmarks, infrastructure), Application (products, integrations, deployments), Economic (funding, M&A, revenue, leadership), Policy (regulation, lawsuits, geopolitics), Safety (alignment, incidents, misuse), and Trend (cross-cutting industry shifts). The masthead filter bar on each edition scopes the page to one tag at a time.

The site is generated and published automatically by a Claude Opus 4.7 agent that reads the current AI news cycle through live web search, fetches each cited article, and writes the summaries. Nothing is republished verbatim. Every daily edition is a fresh research pass; every linked URL is a real primary source.
