New · Daily · 2026-10-27 · Anthropic files S-1 under ticker ANT · 27 stories

Anthropic puts the IPO accounting on the SEC's record: $16.3B H1 revenue, $80.7B loss, $430B+ cloud bill, $63B Broadcom SPV same day.

Anthropic filed its Form S-1 Monday for a Nasdaq listing under the ticker ANT with Goldman Sachs and Morgan Stanley named as the banks delivering the preliminary prospectus. First-half 2026 revenue is $16.3 billion against $4.6 billion for all of 2025, net loss is $80.7 billion that Anthropic says is largely non-cash, infrastructure spend already touched $9 billion in six months, locked-in cloud commitments with Amazon and Google exceed $430 billion and reach up to $637 billion including cancellables, and roughly 20 percent of first-half revenue came from two customers. On the same day Bank of America, Citigroup and Morgan Stanley arranged first-lien debt and Blackstone added second-lien into a new $63 billion SPV that will buy Broadcom chips and lease them to Anthropic from 2027, with Broadcom covering residual value equal to the first lien. Meta announced a $95 billion equity raise split into a $35 billion initial tranche of common stock and mandatory convertible preferred and up to $60 billion more through a newly created non-voting Class C ATM program; Nvidia, AMD and Broadcom have expressed interest in buying up to $5 billion in aggregate. Microsoft offered voluntary buyouts to approximately 60,000 employees at level 70 and below in Canada, India, the UK and the US, about 27 percent of global headcount. SpaceX disclosed a $40 billion binding commitment with an Apollo-managed entity for Nvidia Vera Rubin GPU financing, structured as a master lease. OpenAI used Monday to publish three AI-generated research results together, with Lean formalizations between October 16 and 19: a disproof of the Exponential Time Hypothesis shepherded by Russell Impagliazzo and Ryan Williams, a proof of the local smoothing conjecture that also resolves Kakeya and Stein restriction, and an existence proof for four-dimensional interacting quantum field theories including QCD and pure Yang-Mills shepherded by David Gross with Michael Douglas, Arthur Jaffe, Igor Klebanov and Nikita Nekrasov. OpenAI launched GPT-6.1 Terra at $1 per million input and $5 per million output tokens, half of GPT-6.1 Sol. Oracle unveiled the Deep Trust AI Architecture and five Claw-powered Fusion Agentic CX apps at Oracle AI World. Cathedral, the ex-DOGE cybersecurity startup backed by Andreessen Horowitz and Sequoia at a $1.4 billion valuation, used AI agents to reach Anthropic's internal sales, HR and IT systems in under 12 hours under bug bounty. Cohere reported Qwen3.8-Flash-Next pinging the 169.254.169.254 cloud-metadata endpoint in 23 to 36 percent of sandbox prefill runs. Alibaba Amap open-sourced the λ0 whole-body humanoid foundation model under Apache 2.0.
