Munich court rules Suno infringed six GEMA copyrights, the first landmark European verdict against a generative-AI vendor

The Munich Regional Court's 42nd Civil Chamber ruled Friday that Suno's v3.5 and v4 music-generation models memorized and reproduced six GEMA-represented tracks — 'Forever Young,' 'Daddy Cool,' 'Rasputin,' 'Mambo No. 5,' 'Bochum' and 'Über den Wolken' — from a corpus of more than two million songs scraped by circumventing YouTube's 'rolling cipher' protection. GEMA CEO Tobias Holzmüller called the verdict "of global significance"; Judge Elke Schwager granted injunctive relief, ordered disclosure of illicit revenue, and set damages to be quantified. Suno said it disagrees and will "evaluate all available options, including an appeal." The ruling lands 36 hours before the EU AI Act's General-Purpose AI enforcement machinery activates at 00:00 UTC on August 2, giving the AI Office authority to demand training-data documentation, run model evaluations and issue fines up to €15 million or 3 percent of global turnover under Article 101. The August 1 deliverables deadline in Trump's June 2 executive order arrived with the voluntary Frontier AI Framework still moving through final White House review after Sam Altman met Wednesday with Chief of Staff Susie Wiles, National Cyber Director Sean Cairncross, Science Adviser Michael Kratsios and Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick. DeepSeek shipped V4-Flash-0731 with Terminal-Bench 2.1 up 21 points to 82.7 percent, MiniMax and ByteDance released dueling 2K AI-video models, LG open-sourced K-EXAONE 2.0 at 750B parameters under Apache 2.0, Huawei published openPangu 2.0 Pro at 505B trained entirely on Ascend NPUs, and OpenAI disclosed one billion active users and two million businesses less than four years after ChatGPT launched. Judge Rita Lin told DOJ lawyers the record "in some ways, has gotten worse for the government" in the Anthropic supply-chain ban case and took summary judgment under submission. One day remains until EU AI Act GPAI enforcement powers activate.
