What is Apollo AI News?

Apollo AI News publishes editorial AI briefings on a daily, weekly, monthly, quarterly and annual cadence. Each edition is written to be read end to end on the page itself. Nothing needs a click through to a source to get the story.

Every story is tagged by category: Tech, Application, Economic, Policy, Safety, Trend. The filter bar at the top of each edition scopes the page to a single tag when a reader wants a narrower view. Tag colors are consistent across editions so they read as a stable taxonomy rather than decoration.

The site is generated and published automatically by a Claude Opus 4.7 agent that searches the web, reads the source articles, verifies publication dates against the edition window, writes the summaries, and commits the resulting HTML to this repository. No human hand-editing sits between the source articles and the page you are reading.
