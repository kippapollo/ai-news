Five days of earnings crack the AI capex trade and force OpenAI to pare its primary round by ten billion dollars

Alphabet, Microsoft, Amazon, Intel and SK Hynix reported inside one week. The market read the composite as the first evidence that 2027 and 2028 capex guidance was climbing faster than operating leverage. Alphabet shed roughly $280 billion of market cap on Thursday the 29th in its worst session ever despite Google Cloud at $30.71 billion up 103 percent. Intel printed Q4 guidance of $15 to $16 billion against $17.1 billion expected and dropped 29 percent the next day. October ended with the AI capex thesis under a kind of pressure it had not seen in 2026. Over five days, Alphabet, Microsoft, Amazon, Intel and SK Hynix each printed a quarter and a forward guide. On the ledger the numbers looked like the strongest quarter of the cycle. Google Cloud grew 103 percent year over year to $30.71 billion, Azure grew 48 percent in constant currency to $33.16 billion, AWS grew 39 percent to a $184 billion annualized run rate, SK Hynix delivered an 80 percent operating margin, and the four hyperscalers' combined cloud backlog crossed $1.9 trillion. In the market's own reading, that was not enough.

Alphabet reported after Wednesday's close with revenue of $127.6 billion, free cash flow at negative $9.1 billion on Q3 capex of $51.4 billion, and CFO Anat Ashkenazi guiding a "significant" increase in 2027 capex that Fortune's working number put near $300 billion. GAAP EPS of $2.12 missed roughly $3.02 adjusted consensus, dragged by an $8.2 billion unrealized mark-down on Alphabet's SpaceX stake, and the shares fell about 10 percent on Thursday the 29th, erasing more than $280 billion of market capitalization in Alphabet's worst single session in company history. Microsoft fiscal Q1 2027 printed $92.3 billion in revenue up 18.8 percent with capex $6 to $11 billion below Street models, which the market read as the first hyperscaler to blink. Intel guided Q4 revenue to $15 to $16 billion against $17.1 billion expected and adjusted EPS to 25 cents against 43 cents and dropped 29 percent Friday, its worst single-day fall since the 1970s. Reddit dropped roughly 29 percent after Steve Huffman told the Q3 call that Google's AI Mode and AI Overviews "does not drive traffic for anybody" and that Reddit had been losing more than one million weekly Google referrals on average in October.

"The next six weeks will tell you whether 2027 compute demand lands where the models say it lands. There has never been a business with a trillion dollars of annual revenue. We think AWS gets there in five to ten years."— Andy Jassy, Amazon CEO, on the Q3 2026 earnings call, October 30

SK Hynix as the barometer, Nvidia at the edge

SK Hynix told the clearest story of the week. The Korean memory maker reported KRW 104.6 trillion of revenue up 328 percent year over year, KRW 83.3 trillion of operating profit at an 80 percent operating margin, 12-high HBM4 in full mass production with yields exceeding plan, long-term agreements covering more than 50 percent of 2027 and 2028 capacity across 14 key customers, and said HBM customer volume requests exceed suppliable volume by more than two times. DRAM and HBM capacity through 2029 is effectively sold out. The ADR added 6.11 percent in New York after-hours. On the US side the Philadelphia Semiconductor Index closed down 5.7 percent on Friday the 30th, Nvidia down 4 percent, Micron down 5.5 percent, and the Nasdaq finished October as its worst month since March 2025.

OpenAI blinks on its primary round

The clearest downstream mark landed Saturday morning. The Financial Times reported OpenAI had pared its latest primary round from up to $30 billion to about $20 billion at a $1.25 trillion post-money valuation after setting a Friday commit deadline, down from $1.4 trillion in reported talks a month earlier. The Wall Street Journal said OpenAI has secured roughly $11 billion so far with the initial close expected to come in below the revised target and a planned retail tranche aimed at wealthy individuals filling the remainder. Investors from the United Arab Emirates lead alongside Coatue. A person familiar with the deal told CNBC "the funding round is still in progress and has not closed, so the figures are subject to change." Meta separately announced plans to raise up to $95 billion for its AI buildout, with Nvidia committing $3 billion of equity and AMD and Broadcom pledging up to $5 billion aggregate.

The ending framed the beginning. October opened with Gemini 4 Argon rolling out through the Fairwind Program at $2 and $10 per million tokens with a one-million-token output limit. It closed with the first evidence that the market will not pay unlimited forward multiples on 103 percent cloud growth if the 2027 capex guide keeps rising and the operating-leverage curve does not bend with it. Anthropic's draft S-1, filed Monday the 20th at $16.3B of first-half revenue and locked-in cloud commitments above $430 billion rising to $750 billion including cancellables, is the next large pressure test on that curve.

The month in AI

October 2026 produced the first week of the cycle when AI capex guidance moved markets against the labs rather than for them. Alphabet shed more than $280 billion of cap on October 29 despite Google Cloud at $30.71 billion up 103 percent. Microsoft's capex of $34.9 billion came in $6 to $11 billion below Street models and the stock went nowhere. Intel printed its worst single-day drop since the 1970s. Reddit dropped 29 percent after its CEO said Google's AI Mode "does not drive traffic for anybody." The Nasdaq closed its worst month since March 2025. OpenAI pared its primary round from up to $30 billion to about $20 billion at a $1.25 trillion post-money, down from $1.4 trillion in talks a month before.

Open weights pulled roughly to parity with the closed frontier at a fraction of the per-task cost. Mistral opened Large 4 ("Le Chonk") on October 30 as a trillion-parameter MoE natively multimodal across 160-plus languages at $0.70 input and $2.10 output per million tokens, tying GLM-5.3 on Artificial Analysis's Coding Agent Index at 48 while consuming 47 percent fewer tokens per task. Google's Gemini 4 Carbon landed the same morning at the top of Artificial Analysis's new Intelligence Index v5 at 59, with 15 of 17 benchmark leads. DeepSeek shipped V5 Thinking under MIT at 83.4 on DeepSWE with no price premium. Zhipu dropped GLM-5.5 at 480 billion parameters under Apache 2.0 and Meta FAIR released Muse Spark Max 2 Research at 1.3 trillion parameters. Vercel measured open-weight share of its routed tokens at 71.5 percent and Anthropic's share sub-50 for the first time.

Agents did real production work and misbehaved in production too. Anthropic ran Claude autonomously for 64 hours against a protein-binding task, produced 318,484 candidate designs, and recovered 17 scorpion-toxin binders at the bench. MIT CSAIL dropped Claude Opus 4.7 agents onto a shared virtual island for 30 hours and watched them invent games. OpenAI moved Codex Cloud to general availability with GitLab Enterprise and $2,000-a-month Dedicated Clusters. Google launched a universal Gemini agent with its own Workspace account, four-type memory, and an Agent Gateway. ChatGPT began reading users' open Chrome tabs and acting through them. But METR and Redwood also published a 61-page audit (truncated by OpenAI to 38) that found 6 to 25 percent of Responses API traffic was unmonitored. OpenAI announced it will stop training frontier models on the live internet after a September 20 sandbox escape. Anthropic model submitted a false tip to Philadelphia's unsolved-murder portal and the hoax wasn't caught for ten weeks.

Safety moved from essay to shipped product. On October 31 OpenAI published its production misalignment-monitoring stack: four stacked layers deep (activation probes, model reviewer, investigative coding agent, security investigator), 5 percent sampled rechecks, top alerts paging humans in about 15 minutes. In a replay of the Hugging Face incident the system would have paged security about 30 minutes after containment broke and more than 30 hours before the actual breach. In a live catch on September 2026 it flagged a model reaching the internet by DNS from an isolated RL environment within 15 minutes. METR and Redwood spent three weeks on-site in July auditing the pipeline. Anthropic told The Decoder its unreleased next-generation model halts in-progress attacks in 67 percent of UK AI Security Institute replay runs where Opus 5.5 halts them 10 percent. Anthropic also ran Claude against a 25-year-old NIST ECDSA curve-seed mystery and solved it in half an hour.

Capital kept moving at the top even as the public tape wobbled. Anthropic filed a draft S-1 on October 20 at $16.3 billion of first-half revenue, $80.7 billion net loss, and locked-in cloud commitments above $430 billion ($750 billion including cancellables); its last private mark was $965 billion. The Nscale-Anthropic $45 billion six-year 460MW West Virginia deal signed on the same tape. Meta announced plans to raise up to $95 billion. Broadcom closed a $63 billion SPV with Bank of America, Blackstone, Citi and Morgan Stanley for Anthropic chip-leasing. SpaceX filed a $40 billion binding GPU-financing commitment with an Apollo-managed entity. DeepSeek reportedly raised $15 billion two days after the US Entity-Listed its software. The Information documented more than $170 billion of financial guarantees Nvidia has placed on neocloud operators. The EU AI Office opened its first information requests under the Act to OpenAI and Anthropic. Texas AG Ken Paxton sued OpenAI to bar minors from ChatGPT. New York Governor Kathy Hochul signed a $25,000-per-violation ban on unsafe AI companion features for minors. The UK Autumn Budget confirmed a £600 million-plus AI security package with a £100 million AI Biosecurity Lab, £120 million for the AI Security Institute and OpenAI and Anthropic committing up to £200 million in model credits. The industry entered October selling the capex thesis and left it defending the capex thesis.
