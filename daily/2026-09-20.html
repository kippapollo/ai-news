Meta released the macOS build of Muse Friday, extending the personal-computer-control agent that took the top App Store slot in the United States on iPhone. The Mac client is free and available to US users on a 100-million-token weekly tier, with additional volume available on the paid tier. Muse runs Muse Spark, described as Meta's most capable model in the family, inside a Muse Secure VM in Meta's cloud rather than on the user's machine, so a Mac laptop closing does not stop a task the agent has started. Cross-device continuity is the surface promise: begin something on iPhone, keep going on the Mac, hand off to WhatsApp mid-conversation.

On the desktop Muse reads and writes across five native macOS applications: Files, Mail, Messages, Calendar and Notes. It can pull calendar events, threaded email context, documents and message history into a single context-gathering pass, which Meta positions as the differentiator against ChatGPT desktop and Anthropic's Computer Use. Permissioning is per data type and opt-in. Full Disk Access is optional. Destructive actions like file deletion or outbound message sending gate on explicit approval each time. Users can revoke access at any point from the app's settings surface.

"The team is shipping fast." — Mark Zuckerberg, on the macOS release cadence

What runs where

Muse Spark's execution happens on Meta's servers inside a Muse Secure VM, an isolated environment Meta describes as a "clean room" per user session. A Sentinel agent watches outbound actions and blocks anomalous behavior before it reaches the network or another app. Meta's roadmap names Muse Confidential VM as the next milestone, with user-held encryption keys so Meta staff cannot decrypt the session, though shipping dates for that variant were not given Friday. The macOS release syncs to iOS, Android, web and WhatsApp within seconds. Muse is not self-hosted; the free tier is a hosted tier.

Where the agent lands in the market

Business Insider reported the same day that Muse's iPhone app remains the #1 free app on the US Apple App Store, ahead of ChatGPT, after its September 8 launch. Sensor Tower had put Muse iOS installs at 12 million by September 17. The Mac release closes the desktop gap and gives Meta a full-surface personal agent story before Apple's iOS 27 Siri redesign lands on iPhone 15 Pro and above. Meta did not disclose Muse Spark's benchmark scores, and the model is not available as an API. The company's argument is that the surface, not the score, is the product.
