DeepSeek ships V5 Preview open-weights at $0.14 and $0.56, undercutting Qwen 5 by 80 percent in 24 hours.

DeepSeek pushed V5 Preview to Hugging Face at 09:48 UTC+8 Wednesday morning: an 820-billion-parameter mixture-of-experts with 32 billion active per token, 1,024-expert routing with top-16 activation per layer, 42 trillion training tokens, MIT license. The model lands at 79.1 percent on DeepSWE v1.1, 70.8 percent on OSWorld 2.0, 92.6 percent on MMLU-Pro, and tied 68 percent on CWE-bench, placing it inside the frontier band on four of five benchmarks that matter. DeepSeek's own hosted API prices inference at $0.14 input and $0.56 output per million tokens, undercutting Qwen 5.0 Preview's day-old $0.70 and $2.80 floor by 80 percent and Gemini 4 Argon's $2/$10 by 94 percent. Alongside the hero model DeepSeek released V5 Flash Preview at 180B total with 11B active, V5 Coder Preview trained on an additional 4.2 trillion code tokens, and V5 Math Preview, all under MIT.

Tech · Hugging Face · DeepSeek Blog · 2026-10-07 · Source ↗

DeepSeek pushed V5 Preview to Hugging Face at 09:48 UTC+8 on October 7, 48 hours after Alibaba put Qwen 5.0 Preview on the same platform under Apache 2.0. The release comprises four weight bundles: V5 Preview at 820 billion total with 32 billion active per token, V5 Flash Preview at 180 billion total with 11 billion active, V5 Coder Preview tuned on an extra 4.2 trillion code tokens, and V5 Math Preview distilled from a reinforcement-learned reasoning trace corpus. All four ship under MIT, the same permissive license DeepSeek has used since V2. The DeepSeek paper walks through a Multi-Head Latent Attention 3 scheme that drops KV cache memory by 36 percent versus V4 at identical quality, plus Dense Expert Groups that cluster 1,024 experts into 64 groups of 16 and route top-16 across up to four groups per token.

Pricing is the story that pulled analysts awake inside 90 minutes. DeepSeek's own hosted API lists V5 Preview at $0.14 input and $0.56 output per million tokens with a 92 percent cached-input discount on repeated prefixes. V5 Flash Preview prices at $0.08 and $0.32, and V5 Coder at $0.14 and $0.56 matching the hero model. The $0.56 output number is 80 percent below Qwen 5.0 Preview's $2.80 one day earlier, 94 percent below Gemini 4 Argon's $10 that landed general availability Monday, and 97 percent below Opus 5.6 Mythos's $90 defender tier opened to public API Wednesday. SemiAnalysis's Dylan Patel marked the frontier output floor as formally beneath $1 per million tokens, down from a $20 ceiling the industry held in August.

Benchmarks released alongside the weights put V5 Preview at 79.1 percent on DeepSWE v1.1 (versus Opus 5.5 at 80.1, Qwen 5 Preview at 78.4, Argon at 77.9, GPT-6 Astra at 76.4), 70.8 percent on OSWorld 2.0 (Astra leads at 72.6, Opus 5.5 at 70.6), 92.6 percent on MMLU-Pro, 68 percent on CWE-bench tied with Argon, Opus 5.5, and Qwen 5, and 55.7 percent on ARC-AGI-2 private. Inside the frontier band on four of five benchmarks, well below GPT-6.1 Sol's 58.3 on ARC-AGI-2, and matched to Qwen 5's position on Preparedness Framework cyber at Elevated. Early community runs on OpenRouter clocked 184 output tokens per second on the hosted API versus 142 for Qwen 5 Preview and 97 for Opus 5.5, driven by the MLA3 KV savings landing a 2.1x throughput at matched batch size.

"We opened the frontier output floor at eighty-nine cents per million tokens three weeks ago. DeepSeek just put a thirty-six-percent discount on top of that overnight. There is no frontier output pricing left to defend above a dollar." — Dylan Patel, SemiAnalysis

The release lands inside a weekly cycle that saw Google push Gemini 4 Argon to general availability Monday, Alibaba drop Qwen 5 Preview Tuesday morning Hangzhou time, Anthropic open Opus 5.6 Mythos to public API Wednesday morning Pacific time, and DeepSeek V5 land four hours after that. DeepSeek's blog explicitly flags the open-weights timing as deliberate, with the paper noting that V5 was trained end-to-end on Huawei Ascend 1000 silicon at 11 trillion training FLOPs, with no Nvidia Blackwell in the pre-training run and no US-sourced accelerator in the fine-tune. The weights are up now on both Hugging Face and ModelScope, and the DeepSeek inference stack has been merged into vLLM main as of commit 7c3f9a1.

"V5 is MLA3 plus dense expert groups. Both ideas scale. We will have 24-trillion-parameter weights before Chinese New Year. The question is not whether we train frontier scale. The question is whether anyone pays for inference at Western labs' pricing a year from now." — Liang Wenfeng, DeepSeek founder

Caveats the paper flags: the model's reinforcement-learning safety stack is thinner than Opus 5.5 by Anthropic's Petri auditor numbers on a reproduced reward-hacking suite, and the Chinese-English long-context evaluation at 200k context shows a 7-percent quality drop versus 128k that Qwen 5 and Opus 5.5 do not exhibit. DeepSeek's blog promises a V5 Full release trained to 48 trillion tokens by late November, and a V5 Thinking Mode variant to follow in Q1 2027.
